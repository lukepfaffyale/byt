Why now

Today people have jobs.
Tomorrow is uncertain.

AI is learning to do the work. What it cannot make is you: your taste, your judgment, what you would choose.

People already give that away. Published estimates put phone use at 4 to 5 hours a day, and every tap is sold by someone else. The person is paid nothing and owns nothing at the end.

What we are building

An ecosystem built on verified trust, where players, creators and agencies earn passive income.

1. Play

Tap cards. Each tap writes one block of your Sym: a digital you that you own.

2. Prove

Your Sym must guess picks it has never seen, and beat the crowd. Code gives the certificate, never an AI.

3. Earn

Your Sym answers and works while you are away. You are paid each time.

The economy

How it works

The game keeps 10 percent on top of what a seat spends. Asking 194 Syms a question uses no AI tokens, so the answer that is sold costs almost nothing to make. Play money today.
