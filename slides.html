We propose a fee of 20 to 30 percent by depth, under the 42.8 percent a paid panel adds, and a connector that agencies rent from 49 dollars a month. That supply is worth buying and hard to copy because code gives each certificate and each Sym's data is its person's own. The race must never lower the bar for a certificate, since the certificate is the product. We want profit, growth, and value that keeps growing. Our goal is the Rule of 40: revenue growth plus profit margin of 40 or more. In our base estimate we meet it for three years by growth alone, from 3 agencies to 180, with operating margin below zero and revenue near 250,000 dollars a year at the end.
