# Schema changelog

| Date | Change | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-15 | Ontology gains `topics` (a fixed list). Belief and fact topics are now mapped to it by `content.topic_id`, one belief for each topic. New audiences `view-<topic>`. `CreatorSeat.wanted`, `emerging`. The Guide is sent a narrowed situation (`main.narrow`) | Three ideas taken from VoiceMem (Xie et al., 2026) after reading it, each credited in `docs/SOURCES.md`: a fixed list of topics over memories, choosing the topic before the model sees anything, and groupings that emerge from what is asked for together |
| 2026-11-15 | New column `tendencies.scope` (trait or topic). `TendencyOut.scope` | A belief from one kind of situation was being used everywhere. The split into a trait of the person and a view about one topic follows VoiceMem (Xie et al., 2026), read after the Sym was built; see `docs/SOURCES.md` |
| 2026-11-15 | New table `facts`. Routes `GET /twin/facts`, `POST /twin/facts` (no body), `DELETE /twin/facts/{fact_id}`. `AskOut.offer`, `GuideDraft.note`, `Export.told_twin` | The student decided that talking to the Sym may leave something behind, as structured facts only: one sentence in the Sym's words, checked by code, kept only after a yes. The words said are still never stored |
| 2026-11-15 | Route `POST /gates/{gate_id}/retry` (removes the caller's answers to one skill gate after a 12 hour wait). New card kind in packs: `reflex`, with three reaction packs in `steps.json`. Plan options `retry:<gate>` and `arena` | A stricter pass mark needs a fair second try. Reaction speed games that creators claim and build. The arena is reached from the journey |
| 2026-11-15 | `ResponseOut.cert_need`, `cert_right`. The pass mark for a skill certificate is now worked out for each gate by `verifier.cert_need`: at least two of three right, and a score that guessing reaches at most 2 times in 100 | The rule the student set: a certificate must show a real skill and must never be bought. Before this, a three or four card gate could be passed by guessing about 1 time in 10 |
| 2026-11-15 | Route `GET /me/judgment`. `responses.sure` is now stored for any card with a right answer (strategy and logic as well as pattern) | A Judgment panel in My Sym, and the sureness tap on the strategy games, so the record of sure against right fills faster |
| 2026-11-15 | New column `responses.sure`. `ResponseIn.sure`, `CardOut.bet_sure`, `ResponseOut.twin_bet`, `twin_bet_sure`, `sure_record` | On a pattern puzzle the Sym now bets on whether its owner will get it, and the owner says how sure they are. The record of sure against right is a reference for risk and decisions, as the student asked |
| 2026-11-15 | New table `throws`. Routes `GET /rps`, `POST /rps`. New gate kind `pattern` with two gates (`pattern-four`, `pattern-twelve`); `BoardOut.seq`; `ResponseOut.others_right`, `others_total` | Two games the student asked for: dots that flash in a pattern on four or twelve squares (tap where the next one goes), and rock, paper, scissors against the Sym, with a league where each Sym throws the way its owner throws |
| 2026-11-15 | New table `lessons`. New column `predictions.because`. Route `POST /battles/{gate_id}/learn`. `BattleStarted.sure`, `ResponseOut.twin_sure`, `twin_because`, `crowd_match`, `BattleResult.areas`, `record`. After a battle is scored its picks are no longer held out | The student found that a Sym battle taught the Sym nothing: battle picks were held out for good and the beliefs were never rewritten after a loss. Now the Sym says how sure it is and which belief it leans on, and after the battle it rewrites the beliefs that failed and the change is stored and shown |
| 2026-11-15 | New column `campaigns.depth` (added to an older database file at start by `db.ensure_schema`). `CampaignIn.depth`, `CampaignOut.depth`, and `tiers`, `free_share`, `meet_price`, `reach` on `Agency`. `WalletLine.kind`. A free Sym now costs half the price in a group question; a member costs 1, 2 or 3 times the price by tier, and is paid that | Price tiers the student asked for: start broad and cheap with free Syms, pay more to reach only members, more again for members with a certificate, and most for one Sym. The ledger screen sorts moves by kind |
| 2026-11-15 | New tables `sponsorships`, `autowork`. Routes `POST /agency/sponsor`, `POST /me/auto`, `GET /me/plan`, `GET /world` (no login, totals only). Cert kind `insight`. Transfer kinds `reach`, `sponsor`. The hold for a posted job is now 2.2 times the pay. `ResponseOut.sponsored`, `JobResult.insight`, new fields on `Jobs`, `Agency`, `CreatorSeat`. Leaving now also removes `active_days` rows and the usage link; a free Sym no longer counts as certified | The five loops the student asked for: creators earn when agencies reach their players, agencies back skills for creators to build, insight certificates, a plan for where time counts most, and a Sym that can start work by itself. Plus a public view of the whole economy |
| 2026-11-15 | New route `POST /me/leave`. A player who leaves keeps a `players` row with the nickname replaced by "Free Sym" and the login replaced by a `free-` marker. New audience `members`. `DELETE /me` (erase) is unchanged | The student's rule: leaving closes the wallet and the login; the Sym and what the seats made join the collective, so the game does not lose data or authorship when someone goes |
| 2026-11-15 | New route `POST /twin/ask` and a fourth agent, Guide (tool `get_situation`). No new table and no new column: the line a player says is not stored | The student asked for a way to talk to the Sym instead of scrolling and tapping through screens |
| 2026-11-15 | New table `postings`. Routes `POST /agency/jobs`, `POST /agency/jobs/{job_id}/close`. `GET /jobs`, `POST /jobs/{job_id}/run` and `POST /jobs/{job_id}/review` now also serve posted jobs. `Agency` gained `postings`, `job_pay_choices`, `job_seats`, `hold_times`. New game wallet `escrow`; transfer kinds `hold`, `release` | Until now every job had a made-up requester paid by a game wallet. An agency can now post a job, so work income is paid by a real seat |
| 2026-11-15 | New table `made_steps`. New route `POST /creator/steps`. New content file `backend/data/steps.json` (8 card packs, card kind `logic`). `GET /gates/{gate_id}/cards`, `POST /responses` and `GET /skills` now also serve steps a creator built. `CreatorSeat` gained `packs`, `made`, `step_cards`, `step_royalty`; `SkillStepOut` gained `made`. Transfer kind `maker`. A claim on a step is now across all creators, not per creator | A claimed skill step can be built by taps and played, and its creator earns a royalty from the growth fund when another player passes it |
| 2026-11-15 | New tables `briefs`, `meets`, `one_to_one`. Routes `POST /creator/briefs`, `GET /seats/{seat}/meets`, `POST /seats/{seat}/meet`, `POST /me/one-to-one`. `CreatorSeat` now carries the wallet, lists and past jobs. `Jobs` and `Export` gained the one to one choice. Transfer kinds `royalty` and `crew` | The creator seat is built (survey, test, skill claim, by taps). A seat can pay a large royalty to meet one Sym whose owner said yes |
| 2026-11-15 | New tables `wallets` and `transfers` (a double-entry ledger in thousandths of a credit). Routes `GET /wallets`, `POST /wallets/deposit`. `Me.credits` and the agency budget are now wallet balances. `orgs.budget` now only records what the seat began with. `Vertical` gained `next_gate`, `next_title` | Every seat can add play credits; every move of credits is tied to two wallets; a wallet pays its agents' token fees when it can. A start on an older database writes its past money into the ledger once |
| 2026-11-14 | New table `active_days` (one row for each day a player used the game). `orgs.kind` may now be creator | Agencies and creators need to know Syms have living owners; a Sym's share of data income follows how lately its owner played |
| 2026-11-14 | New tables `consents`, `orgs`, `campaigns`, `earnings`; content file `questions.json` (six fixed questions) | The agency seat and data income: batch questions to opted-in Syms, counts only |
| 2026-11-14 | New table `work` and content file `jobs.json` (three invented requesters). `Me.credits`, `Me.jobs_open` added. Ontology 0.2: definitions, facts and skill trees | Work income: the Sym does a job, the human reviews, code pays play credits |
| 2026-11-14 | New table `self_reports` (opt-in, PII, never sent to a model). New content file `ontology.json` and gate `thinking-1` (14 cards). `GateOut.bin` added | The mirror, bins on the journey, and a map for future gate publishers |
| 2026-11-14 | Built: `tendencies`, `battles`, `predictions`, `certs`, `usage`. Added `battles.gate_id`, `battles.topic`, `battles.twin_kind`, `predictions.player_id`, `predictions.baseline`, `usage.model`, `usage.requests`, `usage.ms`. Twelve made-up sample players seeded | The Sym, the battle and the certs |
| 2026-11-14 | Built: `players` and `responses` tables. Cards are content files, not a table. The other tables follow with the agents | First playable journey |
| 2026-11-14 | First model written: players, cards, responses, tendencies, battles, predictions, certs, usage | Project start; classified in the data dictionary before any table exists |
| 2026-11-18 | No new table or column. The 12 made-up sample players became 193 BOT Syms, one for each United Nations country (`backend/data/countries.json`), renamed in place. Ten of them own seats in `orgs`. `Commons` gained `bots`, `bot_agencies`, `bot_creators` (counts). New route `GET /export/battles.csv`. Starting seat credits 500 to 2000; deposit choices gained 5000. | A living economy to show while few people play; the export was documented but missing. |
| 2026-11-18 | New table `feedback` (seat, moment, answer, day; no player id). Routes `POST /feedback`, `GET /feedback`. New transfer kinds `share` and `share_back`. `Agency` and `CreatorSeat` gained `platform_share`. | Product feedback by one tap with no identity; the game's 10 percent share on seat spending. |
| 2026-11-21 | No new table, column or route. Response model `CodeBase` (inside `SymCode`, `GET /me/code`) gained `what`, `gate`, `prompt`, `picked`, `same`, `of`: what one block of the code was, read from the caller's own rows; `same` and `of` are counts of other Syms and stay 0 when fewer than the minimum answered the card. No name or id of another Sym. | A tap on a block of the code opens a pop-out that tells its story |
| 2026-11-21 | No new table or column. `ResponseOut` (`POST /responses`) gained `sure_gate` (the how-sure record for the cards of this gate only; `sure_record` stays the record over every gate) and `right_choice` (pattern cards: the square the next dot went to, sent only after the pick is stored). `Judgment` (`GET /me/judgment`) gained `unsaid` (right-or-wrong answers given with no how-sure tap), and its line now says it covers all gates. The front end now asks how sure on every board card, not every second one. | A tester saw totals from every gate on a gate's finish screen, with the wrong answers missing because those cards had never asked how sure; a wrong tap did not show where the dot went. |
| 2026-11-21 | No new table or column. `Me` (`GET /me`) gained `people` (Syms with a living person behind them, never a BOT Sym). `Commons` (`GET /commons`) gained `checks_mine`, `told_mine`, `lessons_mine` (with `taps_mine` they add up to `known_mine`). `GET /me/sphere`: the first detail line of an area is now picks out of chances (the share the mirror shows), and the second says how much of the area is drawn (cards seen out of 30, which is `level`); the old line called `level` "percent filled". `GET /me/plan`: a step a creator built is titled "Extra step" and its reason says it is not one of the journey's gates | Counts that read as contradictions between screens: one true Sym count with the people in it, what "known from you" adds up from, one number for an area on the sphere card and on the Brain tab, and a journey that says 14 of 14 gates while offering more to play |
| 2026-11-22 | No new table, column or route. Card content changed, ids kept (card ids, option ids and order unchanged). Reworded: thnk-02, thnk-05, thnk-08, thnk-10, thnk-14, spend-02, spend-08, bt-02, food-02, food-03, food-07, food-08, wknd-02, wknd-04, wknd-07. Retagged as well: spend-02 option b (quality to splurge), spend-08 option b (spontaneous to splurge), food-02 option b (comfort to spontaneous), wknd-04 option no (comfort to spontaneous), wknd-07 option no (comfort to loyal). The Weekend "Sometimes" options are unchanged | A psychologist's review found loaded wording (one answer people wish were true of them), narrow wording (income, culture, diet, sleep) and tags that measured something else. No real player had played yet. Picks made earlier on test accounts on these ids were made under the old wording and should be read that way |
| 2026-11-22 | No new table, column or route. Card content changed, ids kept: the prompts and option labels of 113 text cards were made shorter so a card fits a phone screen. Traits and right answers unchanged. The longer wording is in the repo history before the commit "The game on a phone, and less reading" | Less reading on a phone. Picks made before this date were made on the longer wording, and their times include more reading |
| 2026-11-22 | No new table, column or route. A new third gate, Quick hands (3 reaction rings, cards `qh-1` to `qh-3`), was added: 15 gates and 156 cards. The data dictionary now says what `responses.response_ms` times on each kind of card and names the two values that are not times; `responses.sure` is asked on every board card except rings. The ontology's Profiler entry now says it reads the facts a player chose to keep | A review found the record behind the code. Analysts need to know what a time means before they compare times |
| 2026-11-22 | No new table or column. `CampaignOut` and `BriefOut` gained `unsure`: how many Syms in the group had no real answer (nothing shown, or a tie at the top) and said "I do not know". They are left out of the totals. The number is kept inside the existing `counts` JSON under the key `_unsure` | A Sym with no evidence used to give the first option, which made a total look like agreement. Tonight such a Sym is still paid, because the price a seat is told is worked out before the question is known; not paying it, and asking its person in one tap, is the next step |
